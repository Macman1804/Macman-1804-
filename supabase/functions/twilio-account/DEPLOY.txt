Deploy the Twilio account check

This does not buy a number, send a text, or place a call.
Do not paste any secret into chat, the code, or GitHub.

On a phone

1. Open Chrome and go to:
   https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/bexuyxicdxtviapavdwi/functions
2. Sign in to Supabase.
3. If the page is cramped, open the Chrome menu and turn on Desktop site.
4. Create a function named twilio-account.
5. Replace its code with the full contents of index.ts in this folder.
6. Deploy that function.
7. Open Edge Function secrets for the same project:
   https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/bexuyxicdxtviapavdwi/functions/secrets
8. Add these secrets. Type the values there. Do not send them to anyone.
   TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID
   TWILIO_API_KEY_SID
   TWILIO_API_KEY_SECRET
   MACMAN_ADMIN_EMAILS
9. MACMAN_ADMIN_EMAILS is the email you use to sign in to MACMAN.
   Only that email can run the check. A subscriber with another email cannot.
10. In MACMAN, open Account and tap Check Twilio.

A successful check says Twilio answered and that nothing was bought or sent.
Another account only sees that the check is not on that account.
